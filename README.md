THE IRON VAULT EXPRESS: a top-down stealth heist game on a moving night train. Cross four carriages past patrolling guards and sweeping cameras, steal the Iron Strongbox from the vault cage, and jump off at the rendezvous bridge before the train reaches the terminus. Tools: brake pull, decoy whistle and smoke pellet; the roof route is fast but exposed to the searchlight.
